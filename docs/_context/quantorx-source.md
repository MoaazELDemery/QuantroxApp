## THE PAIN POINTS (NAMED, SPECIFIC — never generic)

The film must NAME specific institutional pains, not abstract ones. Use these:

1. **Regulated industries cannot use cloud AI.** Banks, ministries, hospitals, energy operators cannot send customer or operational data to cloud LLMs. SAMA, CBE, GDPR, sector rules prohibit it. *Cloud AI is non-deployable.*
2. **Chatbots recommend. They do not execute.** Loan origination, license renewal, policy lookup, insurance claim — generic AI gets the user 80% of the way and stops at the regulated step.
3. **Vertical AI doesn't scale.** Sierra is for retail support. Decagon is for ticket triage. Each new vertical = a new startup. None ship a horizontal platform that adapts to the next regulated job.
4. **Knowledge walks out the door.** The senior banker retires. The compliance analyst quits. Institutional memory leaves with them. There's no system that *retains how the work gets done.*
5. **Decisions take days.** SME credit takes 5 days. Merchant onboarding takes weeks. Territory decisions take months. Every day is a regulated workflow stuck in a queue.

---

## THE BENEFITS (NAMED, SPECIFIC — never generic)

1. **Workflows close in seconds.** Not weeks. Not days. Seconds.
2. **The institution owns the intelligence.** Every agent decision is captured as labeled training data the institution co-owns under a 10-clause MSA. The corpus belongs to them.
3. **Compliance is architectural, not retrofitted.** SAMA, CBE, CBUAE, PDPL, GDPR, DORA pre-mapped on day one.
4. **Deploys in 8 weeks per vertical.** Not 18 months. Cognitive Mapping XRays the workflow first; the agents inherit it.
5. **The bank's memory becomes infrastructure.** Senior expertise becomes the institution's permanent operating layer — captured, governed, auditable.

## POSITIVE CONSTRAINT (every sentence must do one of these)

Every line in PEN, ACT 1, and CLOSE must do **at least one** of:

1. Name a **specific institution type** (bank, ministry, insurer, sovereign fund)
2. Name a **specific regulated workflow** (loan, KYC, fraud escalation, claims, license, disbursement, portfolio rebalance)
3. State a **specific number or time** (5 days, 4 hours, 5 minutes, 3×, $64B)
4. Use a **specific verb-as-action** (approves, decides, closes, governs, retains, executes — not "enables", "supports", "facilitates")

Abstract claims without one of these = rejected by reviewer.
